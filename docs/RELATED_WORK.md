## Design pattern already in public discussion

- "Keep the truth in a database; let the LLM only phrase it" has been suggested publicly (owner's
  note, 2026-10-29). **Source link not yet recorded** — to find in the next re-search. Consequence:
  the idea is not new; our contribution is building it, verifying solvability, and measuring isolation
  and its costs.
